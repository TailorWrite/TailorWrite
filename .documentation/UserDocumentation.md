# **TailorWrite User Documentation**

## Introduction

**Welcome to TailorWrite!**  
TailorWrite is a web application designed to help job seekers create personalized cover letters tailored to each job application. With a user-friendly interface and AI-powered letter generation, TailorWrite simplifies the job application process.

**Key Features:**
- Generate tailored cover letters by inputting job-specific information.
- Track, manage, and organize your job applications.
- Update and save your personal skills, experience, and education.
  
This documentation will guide you through using TailorWrite efficiently, from setting up your account to creating personalized cover letters.

---

## Getting Started

### **System Requirements**
TailorWrite works on modern browsers such as:
- Google Chrome (v90+)
- Mozilla Firefox (v88+)
- Safari (v14+)

For the best experience, ensure you have an internet connection and a modern device.

### **Account Setup**
To get started, follow these steps:
1. Visit the Register Page [TailorWrite website](http://tailorwrite-website.s3-website-ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/register).
2. Enter your name, email address and password.
3. Sign in when redirected to the login page.

### **Logging In/Out**
- **Log In:** After signing up, you can log in using your email and password on the login page.
- **Log Out:** After logging in successfully, you can log out by clicking the Sign Out button in the bottom right corner.

## Main Interface Overview

Once logged in, you’ll be greeted with the **Application Tracker**, which gives you an overview of the job applications you have applied for.

### **Navigation Bar:**
- **Dashboard:** An overview of your profile.
- **Job Applications:** Track and manage all your job applications in one place.
- **Profile:** Update your personal information, skills, and experience.
- **Settings:** Customize your preferences for theme and other account settings.

---

## Managing Job Applications

TailorWrite allows you to easily track your job applications:
1. Go to the **Applications** tab on the side bar.
2. Click **Add Job Application** to input a new job you are applying for.
3. Fill out the job title, company name, and job description or paste a seek.co.nz/job URL to have the job title, description and company name automatically filled out.
4. Mark the status of the application as **Applied**, **Interview**, **Offer**, or **Rejected**.
5. Click **Save**. 

---

## Creating a Tailored Cover Letter

### **Step-by-step Guide:**
To generate a cover letter, follow these steps:
1. Navigate to the **Applications** page via the sidebar and click **Add Job Application**.
2. Input the job title, company name, and job description.
4. Click **Generate Cover Letter** and TailorWrite will analyze the job description and your user profile to to generate a cover letter tailored to the job listing.
   
### **Editing the Generated Letter**
Editing generated cover letters was a feature out of scope for this project, but you can copy the generated cover letter and paste it into the editor to make any changes you need.

---

## Profile Management

To update your profile:
1. Go to the **Your Profile** tab on the sidebar.
2. Edit your personal details, add skills, experience, and educational background.
3. Click **Save**  at the bottom of the page to update your profile.

If you’re applying to jobs frequently, TailorWrite can also auto-fill common sections of your cover letters based on your profile details.

---

## Settings

Customize your TailorWrite experience by going to the **Settings** tab at the bottom of the sidebar:
- Select your preference of light or dark mode.
- Save one of your previously written cover letters here to personalise tailored cover letters with your writing style. 
- Customize the template of cover letters generated by TailorWrite.
